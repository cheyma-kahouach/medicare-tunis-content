# AnswerThePublic idea research settings (agreed so far)

Status: settings only. The research run itself is not built yet.

- Language French. Countries rotate (proposed: Côte d'Ivoire, Sénégal, Cameroun, RD Congo, to be confirmed).
- Budget: 3 searches per day, always use all 3. One search is one keyword in one country.
- Rotation: cycle through the keyword list and the countries so the same pair is not searched again before every pair has been searched.
- Source focus: questions people ask AI tools and suggestions from social media (TikTok, Instagram). Search engine style results come last. The Source column of À valider keeps the values IA, Réseaux sociaux, Moteurs de recherche.
- Keywords:
  - Fertilité: sopk, endométriose, fibromes, spermogramme, ovulation, infertilité secondaire, fausse couche, fiv, pma
  - Chirurgie esthétique: rhinoplastie, lipoaspiration, augmentation mammaire, abdominoplastie
  - Optique: myopie, cataracte, kératocône, chirurgie réfractive, presbytie, glaucome
- Filters when adding ideas to À valider: skip ideas already in the sheet or near duplicates, skip price and cost questions, skip diagnosis style questions ("est-ce que j'ai X"), skip other clinics and brand names.
- Every new idea gets Potentiel social (Élevé, Moyen, Faible) and a Rang. Criteria: emotional or taboo subject, myth versus reality angle, a question people ask out loud, relevance for French speaking Sub-Saharan African women and couples, easy to share or save. Re-sort the pending ideas by Rang (1 is the most likely to be explosive) so the reviewer sees the best ones first. Ideas already handled get Déjà traité and go to the bottom.
