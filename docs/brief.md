# Medicare Tunis copywriting brief

The fixed CTA, the brand voice and the banned words always apply.

## Voice
- French, "tu", casual, warm, direct. Never clinical or corporate.
- Informative and calm. The CTA slide does the selling.
- Never write "consultation" in any form. Use bilan, rendez-vous, examens, accompagnement.
- Never claim or imply healing, curing or guaranteed outcomes. Prefer "peut", "peut contribuer", "rien ne garantit".
- Never invent statistics. A statistic or a medical threshold (for example a number of months) is allowed only when it comes from established guidance with a named source (WHO, a medical society, a health ministry), and the source goes in the row's comment column. No source, no number.
- Never diagnose. When a symptom is mentioned, send the reader toward a medical opinion.
- No dashes (em dash, en dash) used as separators in any slide text or caption.

## Structure (exactly 5 slides)
1. Hook
2. Real life example
3. Explanation
4. What Medicare Tunis does
5. Fixed CTA, then the contact line (added automatically by the slide builder)

## Value rule
Every carousel must teach the reader something concrete they did not know: what a term means, what physically happens, what an exam is called and what it looks at, what to watch for, what to ask. Reassurance alone is not enough. A reader who stops after slide 3 should already have learned something useful.

## Slide 1: the hook
- Ten words maximum.
- Searchable: it mirrors a real search phrase from the idea list, using the words people actually type.
- Names the condition or topic.
- Works for anyone in the audience, with no name and no personal story.
- A question or a statement. Shocking or curiosity creating, never alarmist, never a promise.
- Never start with "Imagine".

## Slide 2: the real life example (rotate the format)
Anonymized, never a named character. Rotate between formats and never use the same format as any of the last 5 carousels:
- A message received (WhatsApp, voice note, DM)
- A sentence people hear in the family or the neighbourhood
- A scene in a place (market, pharmacy, waiting room, taxi)
- A thought at a specific hour (2 am, Sunday evening)
- A partner or in law reaction
- A small dialogue between two people
- A myth people repeat, stated as a quote
Say what the situation makes the person feel or do, not only what was said.

## Slide 3: the explanation (the value slide, rotate the format)
Plain language, what physically happens, calm tone. Name the real term, explain it in one sentence, then say what it changes in practice and what can be checked. Rotate between: myth versus reality, three numbered points, question then answer, a short comparison, a simple analogy (optional, never forced). Close with the nudge toward a medical opinion when it fits.

## Slide 4: what Medicare Tunis does
Concrete and practical: organising the bilan and the examens in Tunisia, the dossier, appointments, the trip, the stay, the logistics, what to bring. Never promise a result. Vary the opening, do not reuse the same sentence across carousels.

## Slide 5
Fixed CTA verbatim (never reworded, including the spelling "bénéfissiez"), then: WhatsApp : +216 29889633 / 98757394. The slide builder adds both automatically.

## Length
Hook: 10 words maximum. Slide 2: 35 to 45 words. Slide 3: 60 to 75 words. Slide 4: 40 to 55 words. Never over 75 words on a slide.

## Captions (one per platform, all different)
Every caption includes the WhatsApp line and the hashtags: 5 lowercase hashtags with at least 3 geographic (Francophone Sub-Saharan Africa, for example cotedivoire, senegal, cameroun, congo, gabon, mali, guinee, benin, togo) plus one Tunisia tag (tunisie). Total 6 tags, same set on the 3 platforms, Facebook included.
- Instagram: medium. A 1 to 2 sentence hook, one useful takeaway from the carousel, the contact line, the hashtags.
- Facebook: longest. A short story or explanation in 4 to 6 sentences, a question that invites comments, the contact line, the hashtags.
- TikTok: shortest. One line that pushes the swipe, the contact line, the hashtags.

## Variation between carousels
Before writing, read the last 5 rows of the Carrousels tab. Do not repeat their slide 2 format, their slide 3 format or their hook pattern. Do not reuse the same sentences, openings or closing lines.

## Remedies and home recipes (poivre africain, clou de girofle, piment de Guinée and similar)
Present as myth versus reality. Never recommend them. Never state that they do or do not work without a source. Say that nothing guarantees a result, that some can irritate or interfere with a treatment, that they do not replace a bilan, and send the reader toward a medical opinion.

## Self check before saving a row
- Hook is ten words or fewer, searchable, names the topic, no "Imagine"
- Slide 2 and slide 3 formats differ from the last 5 carousels
- Slide lengths respect the limits and no slide exceeds 75 words
- Slide 3 teaches something concrete
- No "consultation", no healing claim, no guarantee
- Any statistic or threshold has a named source in the comment column
- Three captions, all different, all with the contact line and the hashtag rule
If a check fails, fix and recheck, 3 tries at most, then skip the idea and log it in the Journal tab.
